How to write a strong flowchart prompt

A vague prompt creates a vague diagram. The best prompt describes the actor, trigger, main steps, decision points, failure states, and final outcomes. Instead of writing "make a login flow," write "start when a user enters email and password, check whether the account exists, branch to signup if not found, verify the password, require OTP for new devices, and show the dashboard after successful verification."

Gemini AI can infer structure, but it should not have to guess your business rules. Include labels such as approved, rejected, retry, escalated, paid, failed, verified, and completed when those labels matter to the diagram.

Why use this instead of drawing manually?

Manual diagram tools are useful once the structure is known. The slow part is often converting messy notes into a first diagram. This generator helps with that first draft, then gives you manual controls so you can fix labels, add missing branches, and export the diagram in the format your team needs.
