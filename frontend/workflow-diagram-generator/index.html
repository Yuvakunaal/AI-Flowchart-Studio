FAQ

Can workflow diagrams become system design diagrams?

Yes. A workflow can evolve into a system design view when you start mapping the tools, services, and dependencies behind each step.

Why use Gemini AI for workflow diagrams?

Gemini AI helps turn unstructured process descriptions into a cleaner sequence of steps, making it faster to draft and refine visual workflows.
